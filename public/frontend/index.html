Academic Cycle

Four Phases of Concept Mastery

Our weekly structure ensures no concepts are left half-derived. Scrolling details each phase.

  • Phase 1: Concept Lecture
  • Phase 2: Active Problem Solving
  • Phase 3: Sunday Review Cycle
  • Phase 4: Feedback Loop
Phase 1

Concept Lecture

Attend 3 live classes per week. Live visual explanations utilizing digital whiteboard models. We focus 100% on conceptual derivation.

Phase 2

Active Problem Solving

Download the session worksheet immediately after the class. Complete 5–10 structured numerical problems or derivation essays and upload photos of your answers in the dashboard.

Phase 3

Sunday Practice Assessment

Take our Sunday timed practice test. Simulates real exam board pressures to establish writing speeds, layout spacing, and identify weak topics.

Phase 4

Evaluation Loop

Review handwritten evaluations and grading rubrics returned to your vault. Re-watch recorded sections for incorrectly answered test items to close gaps.

Terms of Service

Last updated: July 5, 2026

1. Enrollment Rules

By registering for an Elevate Tuitions subscription or trial class, you agree to comply with our academic schedules and code of conduct during live sessions. Access key codes are mapped to single user endpoints and must not be shared.

2. Cancellation

Monthly subscriptions can be paused or cancelled at any time through the Student Dashboard. Cancellation requests submitted mid-cycle will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.

3. Intellectual Property

All notes, worksheets, derivations, and high-definition video recordings streamed via Google Drive signed-URL bridges are the intellectual property of Elevate Tuitions. Redistribution or recording of live lecture feeds is strictly prohibited.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

1. Data Collected

We collect essential identifier details including Full Name, Grade, Email Address, and Mobile Phone Number to manage class bookings and dashboard analytics. Password details are secured using cryptographic salt hashes.

2. Storage & Security

Files are stored on sharded Google Drive vaults and accessed strictly via temporary, signed proxy tokens. Student progress statistics are aggregated into database rollups to secure granular assessment histories.

3. Transaction Logs

Payment events and API transaction headers are logged immutably to verify billing events. Financial transactions are brokered by third-party gateways (Stripe/Razorpay) and credit details never enter our servers.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

1. Trial Demo Class (₹100)

Demo class fees are fully refundable up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled slot time. Refund requests submitted inside the 24-hour window will not be eligible.

2. Monthly Subscriptions (₹1,500)

Subscribed subjects operate on a monthly recurring schedule. If a student is unsatisfied, a refund request can be filed within the first 3 days of the initial subscription cycle for a full reconciliation. Cycles that advance beyond 3 days are non-refundable.

3. Reconciliations

Approved refunds are processed via our payment gateway directly to the original payment source. Reconciliations generally settle within 5–7 banking days.
